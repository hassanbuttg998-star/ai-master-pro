Getting Started with FRAZX PRO: A Beginner Guide

A step-by-step introduction to FRAZX PRO: browsing the galleries, copying prompts, using the tools, creating a free account, uploading your own work and joining collections.

By Hassan Fraz November 2026 5 min read

FRAZX PRO is a free website for people who create with AI. It combines a community gallery, where every image is shown with the prompt that made it, with a small set of tools that help you write better prompts. This guide explains what is on the site and how to get the most out of it in a few minutes.

What FRAZX PRO is, and what it is not

FRAZX PRO does not generate images by itself. It gives you the text prompts, ideas and examples that you can paste into the AI image generator you already use, such as Midjourney, Stable Diffusion or Leonardo AI. The galleries are a place to discover work, learn from it and share your own.

Step 1: Browse the galleries

You do not need an account to look around. There are three main galleries:

  • AI Images: portraits, landscapes, cinematic scenes, anime, fantasy and cyberpunk art.
  • Ads Poster: AI-made marketing visuals such as product posters, billboards and social media creatives.
  • AI Stock: nature, animals, abstract art, skies, objects and futuristic scenes.

The posts load as you scroll. Use the search and filter buttons at the top of a gallery to find a style, and the sort button to see the latest, the most liked or the most copied posts.

Step 2: Open a post and copy the prompt

Tap any image to open its page. You will see the full image, its title, the creator and the exact prompt. Use the copy button to copy the prompt, then paste it into your own image generator. Below the prompt there is a "More like this" section with related posts, which is a good way to explore a style you like.

Step 3: Try the tools

Two tools are live at the moment:

  • Portrait Transformer shows tested prompts for styles such as Cyberpunk, Pixar 3D, Profile Photo, Sketch, Anime, Oil Painting, Ghibli, Fantasy and Upscale, each with a before and after example you can compare with a slider.
  • Cinematic Vision turns a short idea into a detailed cinematic prompt that includes camera lens, lighting, mood and color grading.

A third tool, the Background Remover, is planned and is shown as coming soon.

Step 4: Create a free account

An account lets you like and save posts, build your own profile and upload your work. Sign up with your email and a password. You can reset the password later from the login window if you forget it. Your saved and liked posts appear on your profile page so you can find them again quickly.

Step 5: Share your own work

Use the plus button in the middle of the bottom menu to upload an image. Add a clear title, the prompt you used, a category and optional tags. If there is an active theme collection, such as a themed set of black and white portraits, you can add your post to it. Good titles and complete prompts make your post more useful to others and easier to find. Please upload only work you have the right to share and do not upload anything harmful or misleading.

If you make a mistake, you can fix it later: on your profile, open the three-dot menu on a post to edit its title, prompt, tags, category or collection, or to delete the post.

Step 6: Follow creators and use collections

When you like someone's style, open their profile and follow them. In the gallery you can then choose the Following option in the sort menu to see only the posts of the creators you follow. Collections are themed groups of posts that the site team curates, and you can browse them from the Collections link in the menu or from the strip on the home page.

Tips to get better results

  • Study the prompts of the posts you like and notice how they describe light, lens and color. Our guide on writing better AI image prompts explains why these details matter.
  • Change one detail at a time when you adapt a prompt, so you learn what each word does.
  • Save the prompts you want to reuse, and keep notes about which generator and settings you used.

FRAZX PRO is free to browse and is updated regularly. If you have a question or a suggestion, you can reach the team through the contact link in the footer.
